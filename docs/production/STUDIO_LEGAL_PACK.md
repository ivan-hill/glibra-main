# Glibra Studio-Grade Production Legal Pack

This file is the implementation index for the complete Glibra production legal package.

## 1. Production Deal Memo

Captures business terms before long-form documents: party, role, dates, compensation, travel/lodging, rehearsal obligations, credit, expenses, cancellation, and the applicable long-form agreement.

## 2. Location Agreement

Must address:
- location access and authorized areas;
- filming, photography, sound recording, rehearsal and equipment access;
- permitted and restricted activities;
- property identification preferences;
- right to fictionalize the location;
- location fee and separation from lodging charges;
- property care and restoration;
- confidential/private materials;
- owner authority;
- third-party artwork/trademarks;
- insurance and safety;
- release of authorized depiction claims;
- assignment to distributors, platforms, financiers and licensees;
- governing law and electronic signatures.

## 3. Talent & Appearance Release

Must cover:
- name, likeness, image, voice, statements and performance;
- editing, excerpting, captioning and adaptation;
- worldwide media rights;
- promotional/editorial/documentary/presentation/social use;
- compensation and credit if any;
- exclusion of pre-existing IP;
- no obligation to publish;
- participant age/authority representation;
- release of claims limited to authorized use.

## 4. Principal Performer Agreement

Used for lead or featured talent.

Must cover:
- services and performance dates;
- multiple capacities when talent appears as self, musical performer and fictional character;
- recording and publicity rights;
- pre-existing music, trademark, script or character exclusions;
- compensation, expenses and credit;
- wardrobe/props/materials;
- safety and professional conduct;
- exclusivity only if specifically negotiated;
- cancellation/postponement;
- classification subject to applicable law;
- assignment and distribution rights.

## 5. Dance Performer Agreement

Must cover:
- rehearsals, blocking, fittings, technical rehearsals and performance calls;
- recorded movement and likeness rights;
- treatment of dancer-created choreography;
- performance and rehearsal compensation;
- travel/lodging;
- safety, warmup and movement modification;
- wardrobe;
- labor and worker-classification compliance.

## 6. Choreographer Services Agreement

Must cover:
- original choreography deliverables;
- cast teaching and rehearsal;
- revisions and staging;
- ownership model chosen explicitly;
- work-made-for-hire/assignment or exclusive production license;
- pre-existing choreography exclusions;
- portfolio use after release;
- credit;
- cast-recruitment responsibilities if any;
- disclosure of third-party choreography;
- confidentiality.

## 7. Music Rights & Performance Agreement

Rights must be separated, not bundled ambiguously.

Track individually:
- composition synchronization;
- publisher approval;
- master recording use;
- master editing/excerpt rights;
- live performance recording;
- live performance synchronization;
- promotional/social use;
- presentation playback;
- standalone commercial audio;
- samples, interpolations and third-party beats;
- co-writers and publishers;
- master owner;
- territory, term, media, fee and credit.

## 8. Crew Independent Contractor Agreement

Must cover:
- production role and services;
- deliverables and formats;
- commissioned work-product ownership;
- pre-existing tools/templates/software carve-outs;
- raw footage and project file delivery;
- confidentiality;
- equipment responsibility;
- compensation and approved expenses;
- classification subject to applicable law;
- safety.

## 9. Production Confidentiality Agreement

Protects:
- unreleased footage;
- scripts and storylines;
- product features;
- credentials;
- schedules;
- provider information;
- finances;
- technology and business plans.

Must preserve legally protected reporting and non-waivable workplace rights.

## 10. Materials Release

For supplied photography, logos, artwork, documents, archival material and other third-party content.

Track:
- exact material;
- rights holder;
- exclusivity;
- territory;
- term;
- permitted uses;
- credit;
- fee;
- known third-party restrictions.

## 11. Minor Participant Release

Counsel review required before use.

This release alone is insufficient where child-performer rules apply. Production must separately verify permits, trust-account requirements, hours, education, guardian presence and other applicable requirements.

## 12. Production Safety & Conduct Acknowledgment

Covers:
- venue/location rules;
- emergency procedures;
- equipment boundaries;
- anti-harassment expectations;
- unsafe-condition reporting;
- injury/near-miss reporting;
- unplanned physical contact;
- stunts, weapons, pyrotechnics, drones, vehicles and other hazardous activity;
- dance-floor and choreography safety.

## 13. Chain-of-Title Checklist

No production should be marked rights-clear until all required categories are complete.

Track:
- production entity;
- script/story ownership;
- principal talent;
- appearances;
- dance performers;
- choreography;
- locations;
- crew work product;
- materials releases;
- composition rights;
- master rights;
- samples/co-writers/publishers;
- artwork/logos/trademarks;
- stock assets;
- drone permissions;
- minor compliance;
- insurance;
- permits;
- credits;
- immutable executed agreements;
- agreement versions;
- document hashes;
- audit trails;
- counsel review;
- unresolved exceptions.

## Electronic signing standard

The implementation should preserve:
- affirmative intent to sign;
- consent to electronic records;
- exact agreement text;
- agreement version;
- signer legal name;
- signer email;
- signer role;
- signature;
- signed timestamp;
- audit events;
- durable executed copy;
- SHA-256 hash of the executed document.

## Provider onboarding copy

**Media & Production Participation**

Glibra occasionally films hosts, properties, local providers, artists and destinations for travel videos, Connected Travel Waves, presentations, photography and other Glibra productions.

Participation is optional and is not required to join Glibra.

Choices:
- Yes, I’m interested.
- Ask me when there’s a fit.
- Not at this time.

Optional:
- My property or business location may be available for filming or photography.
- I or members of my team may be interested in appearing in Glibra content.

Disclosure:
Expressing interest does not authorize filming. If Glibra proposes a specific production, you will receive the applicable project-specific agreement for review and signature before production begins.
